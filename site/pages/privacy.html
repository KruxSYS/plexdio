Privacy

Short version: Plexdio has no telemetry, no ads and no account of ours. It talks to Plex (your server and plex.tv) and, once a day, asks this website whether there is a new version.

Plexdio and plexdio.kruxsys.com are made and run by KruxSYS.

The app

No telemetry

Plexdio does not collect usage statistics, crash reports, identifiers or analytics, and has no account of its own. Your library cache, play history, settings and logs stay on your computer.

Update check

A few seconds after start-up and then at most once a day — or when you press Check for updates — Plexdio makes one request:

GET https://plexdio.kruxsys.com/api/v1/version?channel=stable&current=0.1.0&arch=x64-setup
User-Agent: Plexdio/0.1.0

It contains only the update channel (stable or beta), the installed version (in the query and the User-Agent) and the package type (x64-setup for an installed copy, x64 for the portable zip). No user, machine or installation identifier is sent, and nothing about your music. Downloading an update fetches the file from this site the same way. You can turn the automatic check off in Settings → About.

Plex

Sign-in, your library and playback go to your own Plex Media Server and to plex.tv, as with any Plex app. Plexdio identifies itself to them as “Plexdio” with a random client ID created on first start. Plex's own privacy policy applies there. Your Plex sign-in is stored in the Windows Credential Manager and is never sent anywhere else.

Optional features that use other services

  • Discord status (off by default) shows the playing song in your Discord profile. With album covers on, Plexdio looks the cover up by artist and album name on Deezer or iTunes, because Discord can't show artwork from your server.
  • Lyrics come from your Plex server.

This website

No cookies, no trackers, no third-party fonts or scripts, and nothing is stored in your browser. The download and changelog pages load the release list from this same server.

The web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, requested URL including the update-check query above, user agent) for operations and abuse prevention. They are rotated and not used for anything else. The server stores no other data about visitors: it only serves the published releases.
