A small, native Windows music player for Plex.

Plexdio is a lean alternative to Plexamp for Windows 10 and 11: a native app with no runtime and no Electron, an offline-first library that opens instantly, gapless playback and a now-playing visualiser with nine retro looks.

Download for Windows Read the docs

Windows 10 and 11 · x64 · Installer or portable zip · No telemetry · Privacy

01Features

Your Plex music, light as a feather

Sign in with Plex, pick a music library and play. Plexdio keeps a local copy of the library, so it opens at once, even when the server is out of reach.

Plex sign-in

Sign in with the Plex PIN flow in your browser. Your password never touches the app, and the token is kept in the Windows Credential Manager.

Offline-first library

A per-server cache with full and delta sync opens immediately. Albums, playlists, mixes or single songs can be downloaded for offline listening in original quality.

Gapless, crossfade, EQ

Gapless playback, crossfade, ReplayGain/loudness normalisation and a 10-band equaliser with presets.

Lyrics

Lyrics in a side panel. Time-synced lyrics follow the song and scroll with it.

Radio and autoplay

Radio stations from a track, an artist or an album, and autoplay that keeps going with similar songs when the queue ends.

Playlists

Create, rename, reorder and edit playlists on your server, pick many songs at once, and keep a mini-player on top.

Visualisers

A spectrum or waveform in the player bar, and a full-screen Now playing page with nine retro looks.

Ride

An Audiosurf-style game on the song that is playing: steer along a road built from its beats and catch the notes.

Cast Experimental

Send playback to other Plex players on your account or server, one or several at once.

02A closer look

Fast to browse

Albums, artists, playlists, genres and Made for you mixes, with instant search over all of them. Everything comes from the local cache, so pages open without waiting for the server.

Now playing

Press V for the Now playing page and F11 for full screen. Nine looks, inspired by the Xbox dashboard, Windows Media Player and the PlayStation's Sound Scope, change on the beat.

Ride the song

Press G on the Now playing page. Ride builds a road from the song's beats and loudness, with notes in five lanes, in a ship, a car or a plane. A beat-tracking model inside the app refines the grid in the background.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64)
  • A Plex account and a Plex Media Server with a music library
  • About 30 MB of disk space, plus the library cache and any songs you download

Good to know

  • Tested against Plexdio's own mock Plex server under Wine and on a Windows 11 VM. It has never talked to a real Plex server or plex.tv.
  • Cast is experimental.
  • Real audio output has not been checked: there is no audio device in Wine or the test VM.
  • The files are not code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn the first time they run.
